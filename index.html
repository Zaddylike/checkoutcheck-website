E-commerce Checkout Quality Assurance

Find Checkout Problems Before They Affect More Customers

CheckoutCheck is a proposed quality assurance testing service focused on identifying and documenting functional checkout issues for small and medium-sized online stores.

Our planned service combines manual testing and selected automated checks with clear, actionable reports that help merchants communicate problems to their developers.

Current status: Planning and pilot stage. Commercial services have not yet launched.

Explore Our Proposed Services

Our Proposed Services

CheckoutCheck plans to provide focused functional testing of important e-commerce purchase workflows in client-approved testing environments.

Shopping Cart Testing

Check cart updates, item quantities, product removal, and order totals before checkout.

Discount Code Testing

Check valid, invalid, expired, and threshold-based promotional discount codes.

Shipping and Tax Logic

Evaluate configured shipping options, free-shipping thresholds, and location-based tax rules.

Guest and Account Checkout

Test guest checkout and registered customer purchase workflows where available.

Payment Sandbox Testing

Test successful and failed payment scenarios using approved payment sandbox environments and synthetic test data.

Order Confirmation Testing

Verify order confirmation pages and transactional email notifications.

Desktop and Mobile Checks

Perform functional checkout checks across agreed desktop and mobile browser viewports.

Issue Reporting and Retesting

Document identified issues with reproduction steps, screenshots, and priority levels, then retest eligible issues after developers apply fixes.

Service scope: Functional checkout testing only. Code fixes, penetration testing, legal compliance certification, and guarantees of finding every defect are not included.

About CheckoutCheck

Our Mission

Our mission is to identify and report functional checkout friction and purchase-blocking defects for small and medium-sized e-commerce businesses.

We aim to provide clear, actionable test reports that help merchants communicate technical issues effectively to their developers.

Who We Plan to Serve

CheckoutCheck targets small and medium-sized e-commerce businesses in the United States, particularly stores without dedicated internal software testing teams.

Our proposed target segment includes stores with 1 to 50 employees and approximately $100,000 to $5 million in annual online revenue. These are targeting assumptions, not verified customer statistics.

Initial outreach would focus on California merchants preparing for website launches, major promotional campaigns, platform updates, or checkout changes.

Our Planned Approach

CheckoutCheck intends to combine focused manual testing, reusable Python automation scripts, and business-friendly reports.

The business is currently in the planning and pilot stage. It is proposed as a remotely operated, founder-led service.
